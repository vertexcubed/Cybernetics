# Cybernetics

Cybernetics is a Minecraft mod for Neoforge 1.21.1, inspired by Flaxbeard's [Cyberware](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/cyberware), adding cybernetic augmentation to the game. Upgrade yourself with various augments, ranging from extra health to double jumps to additional movement. But beware, your body may face the consequences of constant modification...

## Features

- 30+ new cybernetic augments to change how you play the game
- New GUI to manage all of your abilities and augments
- Over 10+ new abilities to enhance your game experience
- Fully customizable and entirely data driven - Modders / Modpack devs can easily add new cyberware to the game.
- A heavy focus on stylish, appealing visual effects

### Planned features

- Custom rendering on the player body - visually see your augmentations and changes to yourself
- Many new cyberware
- Mobs that spawn with cyberware equipped - Cyborg zombies and Skeletons, oh my!
- New gameplay mechanics inspired by Cyberpunk 2077
